# Apply for maintainership

Want to bring your phone back from the dead with us? Official Resurrected Remix maintainers build RR for a device they own, ship it to users through the Updater, and look after those users.

## Before you apply

1. Read the [maintainer requirements](docs/maintainer-requirements.md).
2. Check your build against the [device requirements](docs/device-requirements.md).
3. Release an unofficial RR build for your device and use it yourself for a while.

Meeting every requirement doesn't guarantee a place on the team. We also look at your experience, how you work with others and how you treat your users.

## Apply

[Fill in the maintainer application](https://github.com/ResurrectedRemix-Devices/official_devices/issues/new?template=maintainer-application.yml).

Every issue opened in this repository counts as an application. Issues that don't use the form are closed without review.

Questions before you apply? Ask in the [Resurrected Remix Telegram group](https://t.me/+qLz0jQ_ERZRlM2Ux).
